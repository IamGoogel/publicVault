> [!satz]
> $$\cos(\varphi) + i\sin(\varphi) = e^{i\varphi}$$

> [!example]+ Beispiel
> $$\varphi = \pi: \quad e^{i\pi} = \cos(\pi) + i\sin(\pi) = -1$$
>
> $$\varphi = \frac{\pi}{2}: \quad e^{i\pi/2} = i$$
